This Power BI project analyzes customer transaction data to uncover insights on revenue performance, customer value, purchasing trends, and product contribution. It includes data cleaning, modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboards built around key KPIs like Total Revenue, Total Customers, Total Orders, and Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).

In this project, I analyzed transaction data to identify high-value customers, monthly revenue trends, top-performing products, and location-wise sales performance. The dashboard is designed to be clean, interactive, and easy to interpret for business decision-making.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**

Total Revenue – Overall revenue generated from all customer transactions
Total Customers – Total number of unique customers
Total Orders – Total number of completed orders
Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) – Estimated long-term value generated by customers

**Visualizations Used:**

KPI Cards – Displays Total Revenue, Customers, Orders, and CLV for quick business overview.

Line Chart – Revenue Trend by Month → Helps track monthly performance and identify peak sales periods.

Clustered Bar Chart – Top Customers by Revenue → Highlights highest-value customers driving revenue.

Donut Chart – Revenue Share by Location → Shows contribution of each city to total sales.

Treemap – Revenue Contribution by Product → Visualizes top-performing products based on revenue share.

Slicers – Location and Month Filters → Enables interactive dashboard exploration and dynamic insights.
